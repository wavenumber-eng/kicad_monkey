Rust PCB analytic materializer

Status: proposal. The contract is kicad_monkey.materialization.a0, and ADR-013 records the decision. The wire format and the pack layout are described in the materialization contract.

KiCad source -> resolve physical intent -> analytic materialization -> adapters

Purpose and rationale

Downstream consumers of a board (viewers, fabrication writers, DRC and analysis tools, simulators) need the same facts: what material exists, where, on which layer of the stack, on which net, and how it is transformed. Today each consumer reinterprets KiCad objects (tracks, pads, vias, zones, footprints) and usually polygonizes them, which fixes a tolerance early and loses structure that DRC and analysis can use.

The materializer resolves KiCad's physical intent once. After the boundary it no longer matters whether copper came from a track, a pad, a via, a zone or a filled polygon. Each operation adds or subtracts one analytic shape, under one composed affine, on one stack slice, with a resolved net. Lowering to polygons, meshes or Gerbers stays in the consumer, at the consumer's own tolerance. A separate binding table maps operations back to KiCad sources for consumers that need traceability; geometry consumers do not depend on it.

Layout in kicad_monkey

The layout follows the existing producers. board_plotter_ir is a module of kicad-monkey-core whose contract is authored in TypeSpec and generated into kicad-monkey-contracts; the materializer is laid out the same way.

Contract boundary

  • Shapes. A closed union: disk_2d, rect_2d, rounded_rect_2d, chamfered_rect_2d, trapezoid_2d, capsule_2d, annulus_2d, swept_path_2d (line, arc and cubic Bézier segments with width, cap and join) and planar_region_2d (one outer contour and holes, with the same segments). A disk under an affine is an exact ellipse, so there is no ellipse shape. The cubic segment is the exact form of KiCad's curves on pads, graphics and the board edge.
  • Operations. Surface operations carry an index, an action (add or subtract), a material (copper, dielectric, solder mask, paste, silkscreen), a slice key, a net key, a shape, an affine, and their bounds after the affine. Hole operations carry a shape, an affine, plating, start and end slices, a net key when plated, plating thickness when known, IPC-4761 protection, back-drills, post-machining and castellation.
  • Units and frame. Integer nanometres, KiCad's board frame (X right, Y down). Z is derived from the slice order and thicknesses and never stored. Unknown physical values stay absent; they are never defaulted.
  • Combination. Per slice and material (and per net for copper), material is the union of the adds minus the union of the subtracts, regardless of order. Operations that share a group_key form one item whose subtracts remove only its own adds (knockout text). An optional signed offset_nm grows or erodes a shape by a disk where the grown shape has no closed form; it is never zero.
  • Analytic only. The producer performs no Boolean operations, offsetting or tessellation. Removed material (mask openings, drills through dielectric, profile cutouts) is written as subtract operations for consumers to resolve. Profile contour closure is the one new close-to-format operation.

Correctness rules

  1. Projected and fully parsed views of the same board produce identical results.
  2. A selective result equals the complete result filtered to the same scope (operations, components and diagnostics).
  3. Output is byte-deterministic for the same source and producer version.
  4. Operations on different nets are never merged.
  5. Every operation has a slice. Every conductive operation has a net key; copper without a net uses the empty key.

Products and physical rules

Rules follow KiCad 10.0.6's source (pad.cpp, pcb_track.cpp, plot_board_layers.cpp, pcb_text.cpp, the stroke font and the board parser) and the current KiCad parser where file formats moved.

Stack and profile

  • Slices follow the KiCad stackup front to back; the stackup layer type gives each its role. Thickness, material, εr, tanδ and core or prepreg are copied when stated. Dielectric sub-layers (addsublayer) are slices of their own.
  • The stackup is used only when its copper layers equal the board's; otherwise, and without a stackup, the stack is an explicit default with every physical value unknown (stackup_mismatch, stackup_absent).
  • Board thickness, copper finish, the impedance-control flag and the plated-edge flag are recorded on the stack. KiCad stores no plated-edge geometry, so none is emitted.
  • The stack has one rigid region holding every slice. The contract also describes flexible regions, bend lines and the coverlay, adhesive and stiffener roles for rigid-flex; KiCad stores no per-region stackup, so the producer does not emit them.
  • Board and footprint Edge.Cuts carriers become exact lines, arcs and cubics, chained within KiCad's 10 µm outline epsilon through an endpoint grid. Loops nest by even-odd containment: even-depth loops are regions, the loops directly inside are cutouts (which also covers panels). Unclosed edges and missing outlines are reported.

Copper and holes

  • Pads and vias that remove unused layers, keep end layers or carry zone-layer connections flash where core's board layer flash resolver says, the same one its plotter uses.
  • Pad holes sit at the pad position (the drill offset moves only the copper), are drilled through the board, and use the X size for round drills. NPTH pads keep copper unless KiCad's PAD::FlashLayer rule removes it.
  • Zone outlines may contain arcs. Fills from formats before 20250210 grow by half of min_thickness unless the zone says (filled_areas_thickness no), as KiCad's loader does. Zones inside footprints are indexed; KiCad neither fills nor plots them, so a stored fill there is reported and adds no material. Zone refill is not performed.
  • Via protection is resolved as fabricated: a via's tenting, covering and plugging per side, and its filling and capping, follow the board setup where the via leaves them unstated (KiCad's FROM_BOARD modes). Boards older than format 20250228 did not write covering, plugging, filling or capping, so an absent value is off there. Tenting falls back to the legacy viasonmask plot option, then the board's (tenting ...), then tented.
  • Back-drills and tertiary drills record their side, diameter and end slice, and bore the dielectric between the drilled outer layer and the end layer. Counterbores and countersinks cut copper by KiCad's GetPostMachiningKnockout disk and the dielectric at each slice's nearer surface.
  • Castellated holes are flagged; as in KiCad's drill output they are not clipped by the profile.
  • Plating thickness is not stated by KiCad and stays unknown.

Mask, paste and silkscreen

  • A pad opens a mask side only when its layer list includes that mask layer. Mask and paste margins resolve pad, then footprint, then board; mask margins clamp so the opening stays non-negative; paste ratios apply per axis. In format 20240201 and older a local margin of 0 means inherit. Rules from .kicad_dru are not evaluated.
  • Opening shapes follow KiCad's layer plotter: circles and ovals resize; a rectangle grown by a positive margin becomes a rounded rectangle of that radius; rounded rectangles add an equal margin to their radius; chamfered rectangles, trapezoids and custom primitives are offset.
  • Tracks and arcs on a mask layer ((layers "F.Cu" "F.Mask")) open the mask by their width plus twice their own or the board's expansion. An outer-copper graphic that is also on its mask layer opens it once; a margin alone does not expose it.
  • Text comes from the board plot document, which resolves variables and render caches. Stroke text is placed as KiCad draws it, through realize_newstroke_kicad_a0 (italic shear, line offsets, effective pen, bold multiplier from format 20260826). Text-box text, multi-line text and table separators use the KiCad layout the plotter records carry.
  • Knockout text: the box is the glyph bounds grown by max(pen / 2, height / 9); outline glyphs become holes of the box, stroke glyphs are subtracted within the box's own group.

Nets

  • Net keys are resolved names. The net table lists the referenced nets.
  • Net classes come from the .kicad_pro as NET_SETTINGS::GetEffectiveNetClass resolves them: assigned and pattern-matched classes sort by priority then name, each rule comes from the first class that sets it, Default fills what is left, and several classes form a composite. Patterns are bus-expanded and match as anchored regexes (the common subset of Tcl advanced regular expressions, without a regex dependency) or wildcards. net_settings migrations and old boards' (net_class) forms follow KiCad.
  • Differential pairs follow MatchDpSuffix.
  • Footprint copper keeps its authored net, usually none, as KiCad does. In a net-tie footprint, a shape touching a grouped pad lists the group's nets in tied_net_keys; the nets stay separate.
  • Pad binding entries carry pin-to-die length and delay.

Reported, not modelled

Each case is a diagnostic in the document, never a silent drop: solder_mask_min_width web merging, hatched fills (drawn as outlines), dashed strokes (drawn solid), multi-line stroke text with a line spacing other than 1, outline fonts without a render cache, footprint scale and mirror transforms from format 20260616, net-class patterns beyond the supported regex subset, back-drills that cannot be placed, undecodable zones, material on undeclared copper layers, and zero-width tracks.

Requests, scope and serving

  • A request names products, and optionally slice keys, net keys and a region, a tile size, and limits on source bytes, operations and points. An empty key list means all.
  • Products are pushed into emission. Slice, net and region filters select from the emission with the contract's scope filter, so rule 2 holds by construction and is tested.
  • PreparedBoard::prepare decodes a board and emits every product once; PreparedBoard::materialize then answers any request with the same result a direct call gives. It is Send and Sync.
  • Stages that do not depend on each other (the presentation plot, zone decoding and the other families) run on scoped threads, which are inline on wasm32. Output stays byte-identical.
  • Pushing scope into PcbSelection was measured and not adopted: the net table and zone diagnostics need the same families for any product, and partial zone decoding would change diagnostics and break rule 2.

Safety and resources

  • Source size, operation count and point count are bounded by the request. Without slice, net or region filters, emission stops as soon as the operation limit is passed.
  • Margins and grown sizes saturate at the contract's 32-bit nanometre range; bounds round outward.
  • The validator checks every NonNegativeNm range, closed contours and non-empty paths, slice roles, hole spans, net and binding consistency, the canonical scope, contiguous ordered chunks, and that each shape's anchor points lie inside its bounds.
  • Pack readers bound the header size, check every section against the pack's length, validate the header before reading operations, and fail closed on damaged or truncated input.
  • No new dependency; unsafe_code stays forbidden; the producer and pack crates build for wasm32-unknown-unknown.

Evidence

On the public corpus boards 4-ch-backplane, speedy_processing_module, charge_indicator and taillight:

  • Every board validates, is byte-deterministic, and binds every operation. Rule 2 holds for every per-slice scope of each product, alone and together with copper, and a prepared board's answers equal direct materializations.
  • Profiles close with no diagnostics; the region and cutout counts agree with an independent count of each board's Edge.Cuts carriers.
  • Mask, paste and silkscreen were compared by area with kicad-cli 10.0.6 Gerbers (union of adds minus subtracts): mask within 0.03% and paste within 0.007% on every layer, silkscreen within 0.14% on every layer but one. charge_indicator's back silkscreen differs by 20% because its logo font is not installed on the comparison machine: KiCad falls back to its stroke font, while the materializer follows the board's saved render cache.
  • Net classes and differential pairs equal what KiCad's scripting API reports on the corpus boards and on purpose-built test boards.
  • A pack is about a fifth of the size of the JSON document.

Format gating

KiCad 11 development formats add facts the producer will gate on the file format version: frequency-dependent dielectric models (20260511), pad simulation electrical types (20260521), footprint affine transforms with scale and mirror (20260616, reported today), an exclude-from-simulation footprint attribute (20260828) and microvia stacks (20260830).

Out of scope

  • Zone refill, which callers run through kicad-cli first.
  • Boolean resolution, offsetting, tessellation and meshing, which consumers own; the Geometer gate in the bridge-entry disposition stays closed.
  • 3D models, material properties beyond KiCad's stackup, and device models.
  • Native and WASM transport operations, and a Python API, until the contract is agreed.
