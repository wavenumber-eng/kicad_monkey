# Log: primary-performance-research

Hot-path profile written to docs/plans/logs/2026-07-17-hotpath-profile.txt.

Key profile observations:

- 4-ch full parse is dominated by lexing/parsing: parse_sexp/lex_sexp/tokens accounted for most cumulative time; _parse_pcb_collections and find_all_elements were much smaller in this profile.
- 4-ch projection_nested showed three separable costs: _direct_child_spans / iter_sexp_form_spans scanning footprint source, _rebase_span / _line_column_for_offset spending 31.0s in str.count, and repeated span.parse() / parse_sexp of footprint spans.
- WREN projection_routes showed route-family hydration dominated by per-span parse_sexp and initial top-level span indexing; _resolve_net_ref still appeared as a repeated cost but was smaller than per-object parsing and scanner traversal in cProfile.

Additional candidate beyond issues #16/#17: cache all direct child spans per parent once and filter by head, rather than scanning each footprint source separately for pads and models. Another candidate: reduce per-object parse_sexp overhead for projection family hydration, but that is higher risk and needs design review.
