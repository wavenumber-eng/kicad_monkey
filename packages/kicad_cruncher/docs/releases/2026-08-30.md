# KiCad Cruncher 2026.8.30

Release version: `2026.8.30`

Date: 2026-08-30

This release consumes KiCad Monkey's resolved copper-flash policy for plated
pads and vias and requires `kicad-monkey>=2026.8.30`.

PCB review SVG filtering no longer recreates removed internal annuli from a
via's physical layer span. Per-layer STEP generation likewise uses resolved
copper apertures while preserving plated and non-plated physical drill cuts
through the board, including pads whose authored copper membership omits the
selected internal layer.

Validation covers shared Plotter-IR and native SVG parity, reviewed Yoshi
corpus behavior, inner-layer PCB review output, and cross-package STEP
geometry with drill-offset and through-board-hole cases.
